# Stall brand

- **Logo:** the awning (Stall's roof) over a lime dot (your money, held safely). Files: `icons/stall-mark.svg`, `icons/favicon.svg`.
- **Wordmark:** Bricolage Grotesque with the two l's as the stall's posts holding the awning. Files: `icons/wordmark-indigo.svg` (light backgrounds), `icons/wordmark-cream.svg` (dark backgrounds), both one colour.
- **Colours:** Indigo `#26306E` (main), Lime `#C8F03C` (spark: only on indigo or dark), Cream `#F6F1E7`, Night `#141A3D`, Ink `#161A33`, Slate `#5B6077`, Mist `#E4E7F7`, Coral `#D93F3F` (problems).
- **The wordmark is always one colour:** indigo on light backgrounds, cream on dark.
- **The icon stands alone** next to the wordmark (no box): indigo on light; cream with a lime dot and middle scallop on dark. The boxed version is only the app icon on phones.
- **Fonts:** Bricolage Grotesque (headlines, prices) and Figtree (everything else), self-hosted in `brand/fonts` under the SIL Open Font License.
