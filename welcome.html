Questions

Answers for buyers and sellers.

Do buyers pay any extra fee?

No. You pay the item price, plus the delivery fee if you choose delivery. That's it.

How does Stall keep my money safe?

You pay through Stall's checkout (card, transfer or USSD, via Paystack). The money waits with Stall, not the seller. The seller is paid only when you give them your release code or tap "I've got it".

What if the seller doesn't deliver?

If the seller doesn't move your order on within 24 hours, you're refunded automatically. No forms, no chasing.

What if the item isn't what the photos showed?

Don't give your code. Tap "Report a problem" and add a photo. Your money stays with Stall while the seller answers, and if the item isn't as described, you're refunded in full.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, for free, until the seller marks it packed. After that, if something is wrong, report a problem instead.

What does it cost to sell?

Listing single items is free. When something sells, Stall keeps a small percentage of the item price. Delivery fees go to you in full. Opening a campus store is a one-time fee, shown in the app before you pay.

When and how do I get paid?

Straight to your bank account, as soon as the buyer's release code is entered or they tap "I've got it". If the buyer goes quiet, you're paid automatically 24 hours after you mark the order on the way or ready.

Is the money really there before I hand over?

Yes. You only see an order once the buyer has paid. The money is already with Stall, so you never hand over an item hoping to be paid later.

What if the buyer has the item but won't give me the code?

Tap "I handed it over" and add a photo if you can. The buyer then has 30 minutes to confirm or report a problem. If they do neither, you're paid automatically.

What if the buyer doesn't show up?

Once the order is ready, tap "Buyer didn't show up". The buyer is refunded and gets a strike. You don't.

What if a buyer makes a false complaint?

You get 4 hours to give your side with photos, or to settle it. Stall decides fairly from both sides, and false reports count as strikes against the buyer. Sellers rate buyers too.

Who can use Stall?

Students at Nigerian universities, polytechnics and colleges of education, and approved campus vendors. Students can get the ✓ Verified badge by showing a school document once.

Is my school on Stall?

Stall covers schools across Nigeria. If yours is missing, tell us in the Campus league section and we'll bring it on.

Do I need to download anything?

Stall installs straight from this page in a few seconds and takes almost no space. It also works in your browser.

What are strikes?

Strikes keep things fair for everyone: late cancels, no-shows, and problem reports you lose or don't answer. 3 in 30 days is a warning, and 5 pause the account for 14 days. The same rules apply to buyers and sellers.
