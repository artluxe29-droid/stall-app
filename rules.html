Buyer protection & marketplace rules

Last updated 1 October 2026

Stall connects students and campus businesses at schools across Nigeria. These rules keep buying and selling safe. By using Stall you agree to follow them.

How your order is protected

  • Pay only through Stall's checkout. Never send money to a seller outside the app. Payments made outside Stall are not covered.
  • Keep your chats in Stall. Message sellers with the in-app chat so there's a record if anything goes wrong. A seller asking you to pay them directly or by bank transfer is a red flag: tap Report in the chat.
  • Your release code is your proof of delivery. After your payment is confirmed you get a release code. Give it to the seller only after you have the item in your hands and have checked it.
  • Check before you hand over the code. Make sure the item matches the photos and description (model, size, colour, condition, quantity).
  • Stall holds your money until you have your order. The seller is only paid when they enter your release code, when you tap "I've got it", or 7 days after payment if you haven't reported a problem.
  • Once the order is released, it is complete and treated as delivered.
If something goes wrong: don't give the release code. Open the order and tap Report a problem before it is released (within 7 days of paying). Your money stays on hold while we review both sides, using the order details, photos and your chat with the seller. If the item never came or wasn't as described, we refund you in full to the card or account you paid with. Sellers who mislead buyers are suspended. If a seller can't deliver, they can cancel and you're refunded automatically.

How we check listings

  • Every new listing's photos are checked automatically against its title and description before buyers can see it. Anything unclear is reviewed by a person.
  • Photos must show the actual item for sale, not stock images, screenshots or someone else's photos.
  • Stock counts must be honest. When an item runs out, mark it sold out or update the number.

Sellers must

  • Describe items truthfully, including faults, and deliver what was shown.
  • Deliver or hand over within the time agreed, or cancel quickly if they can't.
  • Never ask for the release code before the buyer has the item.
  • Use their own real name and a bank account in their own name.

Items you can't sell on Stall

  • Weapons, ammunition, explosives or anything designed to hurt people
  • Drugs, including prescription-only medicine, codeine syrups, tramadol and similar, and drug paraphernalia
  • Alcohol and tobacco or vapes, to anyone, on any campus
  • Exam questions, leaked papers, "expo", assignment or project writing for submission, or fake certificates and documents
  • Stolen goods, counterfeit or fake-brand items sold as original
  • Adult or sexual content or services
  • Live animals, human hair or body parts obtained illegally, or anything else illegal in Nigeria
  • Bank accounts, SIM cards registered to someone else, ATM cards, or login details
  • Get-rich-quick schemes, forex or crypto "investment" offers, loans, and betting services

We remove listings that break these rules and may suspend or ban the account, and report serious cases to the school or the police.

Meeting safely

  • Meet in busy, public places on campus during the day.
  • Bring a friend for expensive items. Test gadgets before you hand over the code.
  • Never share your password, bank PIN or OTPs with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from Stall.
